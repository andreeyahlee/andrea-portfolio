About me

A little about how I got here

I got my first camera when I was 10, and I never really put it down. I was always shooting something, chasing anything creative. Still, I chose the practical route: a business degree, a steady job. But the pull never really left. It came back sideways, through volunteering to build sets at church, and that’s where I first got interested in spatial design and event styling.

I’d always been curious about a career in something creative, and UX design is what I chanced upon. What actually made it stick wasn’t the tools or the process. It was noticing how many people around me feel like they can’t keep up as technology, particularly AI, moves faster than anyone can plan for. That’s what pulled me in. The chance to stand in that gap and be part of making it make sense for people, instead of just watching from the side.

Current work

Assistant Manager, Quality, Planning & Global Engagement at LASALLE | University of the Arts Singapore. Spent the last 4 years growing a humble 11 mobility placements a year to 144, with 139 institutional partnership agreements anchored, 82 active partnerships across four global regions. I’ve also recently completed a part-time UX Design Immersive at General Assembly and have been building myself as a product designer, one case study at a time.

How I work

Before I open Figma, I classify the problem — seeking out design problems, product problems, people problems. They need different solutions, and confusing them wastes everyone’s time. I learned this the hard way running institutional systems for a decade. Listen first, map the system, then build. I practice Pilates, which has taught me more about good work than I expected: control before range, foundation before flourish.

What I build at home

My mum looks after my grandfather, and the tools available to her aren’t good enough. That’s where the caregiver coordination app I’m building started — not from a persona or a market gap, but from watching someone I love navigate something unnecessarily hard. I’m also building an animated values reel for my nephew, who is five. Same principle, different brief: earn the attention first, then make it worth it.

Career

Ran Magnolia & Pine for four years alongside a full-time role and successfully delivered on events for clients like Guerlain, LVMH, CPF Board — where success meant nobody noticed how much went into it. Closed it because doing two demanding things well has an expiry date, and I chose. Before that: PSB Academy, Temasek Foundation Cares, and a year in Brazil that taught me more about listening across a language barrier than anything since.
